# Paydirt: Soil & Gold Assay
Find the hidden pay streak (3–5 connected cells) and hit the gold quota in 60 seconds — or the run is over. A 60-second browser puzzle, not the Steam / Google Play tycoon of the same name.
Probe a tile to read how many of its neighbours hold gold: 0 is cold, 1–2 is warm, 3–4 is hot. You get 5 Soil Probes per ante — they break no ground. Keys: P picks the Probe, S picks the Shovel; right-click a tile to dig it.
Dig a tile with the Assay Shovel to pull its gold out of the ground. You get 3 digs per ante.
Gold is weighed in grams and troy ounces (1 troy oz = 31.1035 g). Best ante reached and best run total are saved on this device.
Gold quota per ante: Ante 1: 12.0 g, Ante 2: 16.8 g, Ante 3: 23.5 g, then ×1.4 each ante. One cell in each streak is the mother lode (6–8 g at Ante 1); the rest are ordinary nuggets (3.5–6 g).
Hit the quota at the weigh-in and you advance to the next ante with a fresh grid and a bigger quota. Miss it and the run ends on the spot.
A good run probes to map the pay streak, then digs the richest cells. A probe that reads 2 with only 2 untouched neighbours means both hold gold.
Free to play: no purchases, no ads, no account. Open index.html in any desktop or mobile browser, or host the files on any static host — no server or build step. No daily seed, shop, crews, or collections — the 60-second loop is the whole game.
Restart any time with the ↻ button in the header; it reseeds the claim and resets the timer, tools, and score. Rules are covered by `node test.js`; the 5-person playtest plan is in PLAYTEST.md.
