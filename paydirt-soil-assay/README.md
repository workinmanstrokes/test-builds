# Paydirt: Soil & Gold Assay
Find the hidden pay streak and hit the gold quota in 60 seconds — or the run is over.

Ante rules: every round ("Ante") is a fresh 60-second dig on a new 6x6 claim with a hidden pay streak of 3-5 connected cells. You get 5 Soil Probes and 3 Shovel digs per ante — the timer and the dig budget are the constraints.
Probe a tile to read how many of its neighbours hold gold — it breaks no ground.
Dig a tile with the Assay Shovel to pull its gold out of the ground.
Gold quota per ante: Ante 1: 12.0 g, Ante 2: 16.8 g, Ante 3: 23.5 g, Ante 4: 32.9 g, Ante 5: 46.1 g, Ante 6: 64.5 g, Ante 7: 90.4 g, Ante 8: 126.5 g, then x1.4 each ante.
Per-cell gold also grows with the ante (x1.35), so a good player keeps up for a few antes. One cell in each streak is the mother lode (6-8 g at Ante 1); the rest are ordinary nuggets (3.5-6 g at Ante 1). The streak is longest at Ante 1 (5 cells) and shrinks to 3 cells by Ante 5.
Hit the quota at the weigh-in and you advance to the next ante with a fresh grid and a bigger quota. Miss it and the run ends on the spot.
Best ante reached and best run total gold are saved on this device.
A good run probes to map the pay streak, then digs the richest cells. A probe that reads 2 with only 2 untouched neighbours means both hold gold.
Open index.html in any desktop or mobile browser — no install, no server, no account.
