## Description
Period 8 is a hand-drawn school anomaly game. The hallway outside Room 8, and Room 8 itself, keep repeating. Look
around every corner: the lockers, the bulletin board, the founder's portrait, the chalkboard, the kids at their desks.

If everything is exactly as you remember, keep going. If anything is different, even slightly, turn back.
Get eight loops right in a row and the final bell rings. Miss one and the count resets, and something comes for you.

- 44 anomalies, from obvious to "wait, was that always there?"
- Three endings, including a secret one for the sharpest eyes
- Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, tap to inspect
- Gore-free scares, with a gentle-scare option
- Spot the Anomaly in 3 Seconds: a quick-fire vertical mode made for Shorts and TikTok
- Rewarded ads only (hints and saving your streak), never forced
