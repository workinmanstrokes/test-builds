Privacy Policy – Period 8

Developer: Diddlerrr · Contact: mscofield1588@gmail.com · Effective: October 16, 2026

Period 8 is a gore-free school mystery/horror game intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any account.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (current run, endings found, anomaly journal, best scores and settings) is saved only on your device using local storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. We cannot see or recover it.

Screenshots

If you use the in-game screenshot tool, the picture is created on your device and saved or shared only where you choose (for example your gallery or a messaging app). We never receive it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (a hint, or keeping your streak after a mistake). There are no forced ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, or under US state privacy laws) you are asked for consent through Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice anytime from Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics

The app does not currently use any analytics service. If we add one (for example Google Firebase Analytics, to understand in aggregate how far players get), we will update this policy first.

In-app purchases

An optional "Remove ads" purchase may be offered through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help delete it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the service provider above (Google) to operate ads.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalized ads via Ad privacy choices or Android settings.
  • Delete all game data by clearing app storage, uninstalling, or Settings > Reset progress.
  • Contact us at the email above with any privacy request.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
