Privacy Policy – Print Doctor

Effective October 23, 2026. Developer: DowntownDiddlerrr.

Print Doctor collects no data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads, or servers. Your print log, including any photos you take or choose, is stored only on your device (IndexedDB) and is never transmitted. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it.

The camera is used only when you choose to attach a photo to a log entry.
