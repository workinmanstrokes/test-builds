# Lure Economy — Michigan Lunker Rush (one page)

## Coins

Earned only by landing fish: **coins = weight (lb) × species coin value.**

| Species | Coin value per lb |
|---|---|
| Bluegill / Pumpkinseed / Yellow Perch | 8 |
| Largemouth Bass | 15 |
| Smallmouth Bass | 16 |
| Walleye | 18 |
| Northern Pike | 20 |

- Trophy fish (≥ 90% of the species' max weight) pay **1.5×**.
- Rare gold fish (4% of spawns, shown gold when the drone reveals them) pay **3×**.
- Daily target (one named fish per day, shown on the menu): **+50 coins** when landed.
- Coins are saved on the device only. There is no purchase with real money anywhere in the game.

## Lures — virtual only

Lures are **virtual items** "printed" by the in-game 3D printer in the Workbench.
They exist only inside the game: no real lure is printed, shipped, or sold,
and there is no real-money path to them. **Physical 3D-printed lure
redemption was cut from the plan** until the core loop retains players
(VEX items 5 and 15) — cost, fulfilment, and any real-money path are
deliberately unaddressed until then.

| Lure | Cost | What it changes in play |
|---|---|---|
| Texas-Rigged Senko | free (owned) | Bite rate ×1.6 largemouth & smallmouth bass |
| Topwater Frog | 150 | Bite rate ×1.8 pike, ×1.3 bass |
| Squarebill Crankbait | 300 | Bite rate ×1.6 walleye, ×1.5 pike, ×1.2 bass |

A lure changes **what bites**, never whether the loop works: every fish can
be caught on the free Senko.

## Line — what stronger line changes

| Line | Cost | Snap tension | Tension gain while a fish runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 lb Braid | free (owned) | 100 | ×1.00 |
| 25 lb Fluoro Leader | 200 | 125 | ×0.75 |
| 40 lb Heavy Braid | 400 | 155 | ×0.55 |

Stronger line does not make fish bite more — it only forgives harder runs,
so it is the answer to pike, not to panfish.

## Earn rate (so the prices mean something)

A typical landed fish pays 8–30 coins. The Frog costs ~6–10 good catches;
the Crankbait ~12–20. Lures are a goal for an evening of fishing, not a
grind wall: the first lake is fully fishable with the free Senko and
15 lb braid.

## What coins do NOT buy

Lakes, species, weather, the drone, gold fish, or the daily target. All
three lakes are open from the start and every fish can be caught on free
gear. Coins buy lures and line, nothing else.
