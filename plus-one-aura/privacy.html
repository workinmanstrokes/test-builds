Privacy Policy – +1 Aura: Tap to Ascend

Developer: Diddlerrr · Contact: mscofield1588@gmail.com · Effective: October 15, 2026

+1 Aura is a casual tapping game intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any account.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (aura, upgrades, stages, settings) is saved only on your device using local storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. We cannot see or recover it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (for example a 2x boost). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, or under US state privacy laws) you are asked for consent through Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice anytime from Settings > Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

If enabled, the app uses Firebase Analytics to understand gameplay in aggregate, such as app opens, return visits, stages reached, level-ups and whether rewarded ads were watched. This includes an app-instance identifier and device information (model, OS version, country derived from IP). It does not include your name or contact details. See Firebase privacy information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help delete it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers above (Google) to operate ads and analytics.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalized ads via the Ad privacy option or Android settings.
  • Delete all game data by clearing app storage or uninstalling.
  • Contact us at the email above with any privacy request.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
