Privacy Policy: +1 Aura: Tap to Ascend

Developer: Diddlerrr Contact: mscofield1588@gmail.com App: +1 Aura: Tap to Ascend (Android package com.downtowndiddlerrr.taptoascend) Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the +1 Aura Android app ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. +1 Aura is a casual tapping game for players aged 13 and older.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • The app uses two Google services: Google AdMob for optional rewarded video ads and Google Firebase Analytics for anonymous gameplay statistics. These services collect some device and usage information as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (Aura, upgrades, stages, chosen skin, settings such as sound and vibration, and counters that limit how often optional ads are offered) is saved in the app's local storage on your device. We never receive it and cannot view or restore it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include this data in your own Google account backup. That backup is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus (a 2x boost, reviving a lost streak, skipping to the next world, or doubling offline earnings). There are no forced or pop-up ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect: - device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID; - your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location (country/region); - ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped); - device and app information and diagnostic data (device model, OS version, app version, performance data).

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads, to personalise ads where permitted, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, or under US state privacy laws), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options in the app. You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the game is played so we can improve it. It records events such as: the app being opened, return visits by day, stages unlocked, level-ups, overheats, upgrades bought, skins chosen, and whether optional ads were offered or completed. Along with these events, Firebase collects an app-instance identifier, the Advertising ID where available, device information (model, OS version, language), app version, and approximate location (country) derived from your IP address.

Analytics events do not include your name, contact details or anything you type. We use analytics only to improve the game; we do not use it to identify you.

Analytics collection is off by default. It is switched on only after you give consent through Google's consent tool (where consent is required) and only while Settings > Share anonymous analytics is on. You can turn it off at any time in Settings; collection stops immediately.

Learn more: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3a. Leaderboards and friends (Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore)

If online leaderboards are available, the app signs you in anonymously (no email, phone or account needed) and stores on Google Firebase servers: a random user ID, the player name and emoji avatar you choose, a random 6-character friend code, the user IDs of friends you added by code, and your best scores (all-time aura, highest tower floor, best Speed Escape distance) with the time they were sent. Your player name, avatar and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards. Please don't use your real name. Names are filtered for offensive words.

Sharing a score uses your device's share sheet; nothing is sent until you pick an app. A challenge link contains your player name, friend code and the score. To delete your leaderboard data, email us your friend code (Ranks > Friends) and we'll remove it within 30 days.

4. In-app purchases (if offered)

The app may offer an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment card or billing details. The app checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase so it can unlock the benefit, and records anonymous analytics events when a purchase starts, completes or fails.

5. Permissions

The app requests internet access (to load ads and send analytics) and uses vibration for haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings. Google's ad SDK uses the Advertising ID permission. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for money. Information is collected by, and shared with, Google (AdMob and Firebase) only to provide ads, analytics and leaderboards as described above. Your player name, avatar and scores are shown to other players. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Data retention

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Firebase Analytics data is retained according to the data-retention setting of our Google Analytics property (2 months, the Google Analytics default). AdMob data is retained by Google according to Google's policies.

8. Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No data is stored on servers operated by us; we do not run any servers for this app.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and information was collected, contact us and we will help have it deleted.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Ads and consent: change your consent choices in Settings > Privacy options (where shown), or reset/delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Analytics: switch off Settings > Share anonymous analytics at any time.
  • Game data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Requests: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of information about you. Because we don't collect your name or contact details, we may need your Firebase app-instance ID or Advertising ID to locate data. Email mscofield1588@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

11. International transfers

Google may process information in the United States and other countries where it operates, using safeguards described in Google's privacy policy.
