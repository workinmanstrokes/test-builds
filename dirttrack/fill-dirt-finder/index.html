How to play

Build spec: Paydirt: Fill-Dirt Finder is a 60-second timed digging game. You dig — tap, click, or drag — across a grid of dirt cells. Ordinary dirt clears empty; hidden pay-dirt cells flash gold flecks and add a nugget to your haul. Reach the site's target gold weight before the timer ends to succeed; run out of time below target and the run fails, with your haul still shown. Three graded sites (Creek Bed, Old Yard, River Claim) get bigger and richer, and your best haul per site is saved in this browser.

Controls: mouse — click or click-and-drag. Touch — tap or drag a finger. Every cell is at least 44 px, so it works on a phone without zooming. The timer is driven by the clock, not by animation frames, so switching apps or a stalled frame cannot freeze or extend a run; the run always ends on time. If saving your best haul fails on this browser, the result screen says so instead of failing silently.

Free entry. No ads, no subscription, no purchases. Single self-contained page — under 2 seconds to playable on a normal connection.
