Final Cast Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · Developer: Diddlerrr

Final Cast is a single-player fishing game made by Diddlerrr. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it on Android or on the web.

Information we collect

None. Final Cast does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Your saved game

Your progress (gold, level, gear, quests and catch log) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time with Log → Reset save, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The Android app requests internet access (a standard permission for apps that display web content; the game itself runs offline and sends nothing) and vibration (haptic feedback when a fish bites or the line strains). It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Links to other sites

After you finish every quest the game shows a link to the Fishing Almanac website. Following it opens your browser, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Final Cast is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
