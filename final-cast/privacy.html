Final Cast Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026 · Developer: Diddlerrr

Final Cast is a single-player fishing game made by Diddlerrr. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it on Android or on the web.

Information we collect

Final Cast does not require an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, or other personal information. There are no ads. Your fishing progress stays on your device.

When you play on the public web or in the Android app, and your browser or device is not sending Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the game loads two limited tools so we can fix crashes and see how often the game is opened. Both are labeled with the game name Final Cast.

Crash reports (Sentry). If the game hits an error, a report may be sent with the error text and a short trail of what the game was doing. Reports do not include your name, an account, or a user id. Text that looks like an email address is removed before the report is sent. Taps and anything you type are not recorded.

Anonymous play stats (PostHog). We count that a visit started and ended, so we can see that Final Cast was opened. We do not record the screen, session replay, clicks, or what you type. You are not identified, and these stats are not attached to a person profile. An anonymous id for those stats is stored on your device only.

Your saved game

Your progress (gold, level, gear, quests and catch log) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage, under a key the game uses for the save. It is not sent with crash reports or play stats. You can erase the save at any time with Log → Reset save. Clearing the app's storage, clearing site data, or uninstalling the app also removes the save and the anonymous stats id.

Permissions

The Android app requests internet access so it can send those anonymous crash reports and play stats when you are online, and vibration (haptic feedback when a fish bites or the line strains). The game still plays with no connection; the reports are skipped when they cannot be sent. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Links to other sites

After you finish every quest the game shows a link to the Fishing Almanac website. Following it opens your browser, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Final Cast is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
