Privacy Policy: Period 8

Developer: Diddlerrr
Contact: mscofield1588@gmail.com
App: Period 8 (Android package com.diddlerrr.period8)
Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the Period 8 Android app ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. Period 8 is a gore-free school mystery/horror game for players aged 13 and older.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • Screenshots you make in the game stay on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • The app uses two Google services: Google AdMob for optional rewarded video ads and Google Firebase Analytics for anonymous gameplay statistics. These services collect some device and usage information as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your progress (current run, endings found, anomaly journal, best scores, settings such as sound, vibration and jump-scare intensity, and counters that limit how often optional ads are offered) is saved in the app's local storage on your device. We never receive it and cannot view or restore it. You can erase it at any time with Settings > Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling. If Android's backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your own Google account backup, which is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

2. Screenshots and clip results

The in-game screenshot button and the clip-mode "Save result card" button create an image on your device. The app saves it temporarily in its own cache and opens Android's share sheet so you can save or send it wherever you choose (for example your gallery or a messaging app). We never receive these images. Anything you share is then handled by the app or service you share it with.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a hint or for keeping your streak after a mistake. There are no forced or pop-up ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect:
- device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID;
- your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location (country/region);
- ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped);
- device and app information and diagnostic data (device model, OS version, app version, performance data).

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads, to personalise ads where permitted, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, or under US state privacy laws), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options in the app (shown where required). You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the game is played so we can improve it, for example: the app being opened, return visits by day, loops started, whether each loop was answered correctly and which anomaly it was, hints used, which ending was reached, use of clip mode and the screenshot tool, and whether optional ads were offered or completed. Along with these events, Firebase collects an app-instance identifier, the Advertising ID where available, device information (model, OS version, language), app version, and approximate location (country) derived from your IP address.

Analytics is off until you allow it. Firebase Analytics starts disabled and nothing is sent until Google's consent tool returns a result that allows it (where consent is required) and you have not turned off Settings > Share anonymous gameplay stats. You can switch that setting off at any time; collection then stops immediately. Where consent is required you can also change your choices from Settings > Privacy options.

Analytics events do not include your name, contact details, screenshots or anything you type. We use analytics only to improve the game; we do not use it to identify you.

Learn more: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. In-app purchases

The current version of the app has no in-app purchases. If an optional "Remove ads" purchase is added in the future, payments will be processed entirely by Google Play, we will never receive your payment details, and this policy will be updated.

6. Permissions

The app requests internet access (to load ads and send analytics) and uses vibration for haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings. Google's ad SDK uses the Advertising ID permission. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for money. Information is collected by, and shared with, Google (AdMob and Firebase) only to provide ads and analytics as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. Data retention

Progress stays on your device until you delete it. Firebase Analytics data is retained according to the data-retention setting of our Google Analytics property (2 months, Google Analytics' default). AdMob data is retained by Google according to Google's policies.

9. Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We don't run any servers for this app, and no data is stored on servers operated by us.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and information was collected, contact us and we will help have it deleted.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Ads: change ad consent in Settings > Privacy options (where shown), or reset/delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Analytics: turn off Settings > Share anonymous gameplay stats; where consent is required, you can also withdraw it in Settings > Privacy options.
  • Game data: Settings > Reset progress, clear the app's storage, or uninstall.
  • Scares: switch jump scares to Gentle in Settings.
  • Requests: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of information about you. Because we don't collect your name or contact details, we may need your Firebase app-instance ID or Advertising ID to locate data. Email mscofield1588@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

12. International transfers

Google may process information in the United States and other countries where it operates, using safeguards described in Google's privacy policy.

13. Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
