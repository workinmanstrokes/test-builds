Privacy Policy — Brainrot: Tactical Aura Rush

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what data Brainrot: Tactical Aura Rush ("the App") collects and how it is used. The App is developed by Diddlerrr, contact: mscofield1588@gmail.com.

Data the App itself collects

The App does not require an account, login, or personal information to play, and the App itself does not transmit any personal data anywhere.

The App saves a small amount of gameplay data on your device only: your best aura score, the furthest zone you have reached, your Essence balance and permanent upgrade levels, and your sound and vibration settings. This data never leaves your device.

Advertising

The App does not display ads and does not include any advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for users 13 and older, is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect children's data.

Data deletion

Since the App stores data only locally on your device, uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes all data the App has stored.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
