Privacy Policy — Aether Surge: Survivor RPG

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what data Aether Surge: Survivor RPG ("the App") collects and how it is used. The App is developed by Diddlerrr, contact: mscofield1588@gmail.com.

Data the App itself collects

The App does not require an account, login, or personal information to play. The App itself does not send your gameplay data to us or to any server we run.

The App saves gameplay data on your device only (local storage): best scores and run records, gold, gems and Aether Motes, unlocked heroes and gear and their upgrade levels, chest timers, mission and tower progress, how many optional ads you watched today (used only to cap them), and your sound, graphics and screen-shake settings. This data stays on your device. The App does not use Firebase or any cloud save, and has no online leaderboards or chat.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads, which only play when you tap a button offering a reward (for example a revive or a bonus XP chest), and a small banner ad on the menu and results screens. There are no interstitial (full-screen pop-up) ads. Versions of the game played on web game portals show only ads served by that portal under its own privacy policy.

When an ad is requested, Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, may collect and process data from your device, including your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate general location), device and app information, and information about ad interactions. Google uses this data to deliver, measure and limit ads, to prevent fraud, and, where you allow it, to show personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

See how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and certain US states. Your choices control whether ads may be requested and whether they can be personalized. If a privacy choice applies to you, a Privacy options button appears on the title screen so you can review or change your choices at any time.

Purchases

If in-app purchases (such as removing ads) are offered, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; we only learn on your device that a purchase is active.

Analytics

The App does not include any analytics SDK.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for users 13 and older, is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes all gameplay data the App stored on your device. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's policies linked above; you can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings. For any question or request, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
