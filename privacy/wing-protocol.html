Privacy Policy: Wing Protocol

Developer: Diddlerrr
Contact: mscofield1588@gmail.com
App: Wing Protocol (Android package com.diddlerrr.wingprotocol)
Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the Wing Protocol Android app ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. Wing Protocol is a mech survival shooter for players aged 13 and older.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.
  • There are no real-money purchases. Everything in the game is earned by playing.
  • We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your progress (unlocked mechs, gear, credits, keys, cores and shards, mission progress, best Tower floor, crate timers and settings such as sound and graphics quality) is saved in the app's local storage on your device. We never receive it and cannot view or restore it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If Android's backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your own Google account backup, which is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

2. Sponsor Crate

The Store's optional Sponsor Crate plays a short in-game countdown and then grants the crate. In this version it does not load or show third-party ads, and no information is sent to any ad network. If real ads are ever added, we will update this policy, ask for consent where the law requires it, and update the app's Google Play Data safety section before that version is released.

3. Fonts loaded from Google

When the app has an internet connection it downloads its typefaces (Rajdhani and IBM Plex Sans) from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this sends your device's IP address and basic browser information (for example the WebView version) to Google so the fonts can be delivered. The request does not include any game data or identifiers from us, and Google states that it does not use Google Fonts requests to build profiles or target ads. If there is no connection, the app uses your device's built-in fonts and works fully offline. Learn more: https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy

4. In-app purchases

The current version of the app has no in-app purchases. Crates are opened with in-game currency earned by playing, and the drop odds are shown on every crate. If purchases are ever added, payments will be processed entirely by Google Play, we will never receive your payment details, and this policy will be updated first.

5. Permissions

The app requests only internet access (used for the fonts above). It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or advertising ID permissions.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone. We may disclose information if required by law, but because we don't collect any, there is normally nothing to disclose.

7. Data retention and security

Progress stays on your device until you delete it. We don't run any servers for this app, and no data is stored on servers operated by us. Network requests the app makes use HTTPS.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Game data: clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Requests: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access or delete information about you. We hold none, but you can email mscofield1588@gmail.com with any question and we'll respond within 30 days.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes (for example if ads or analytics are added). The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
