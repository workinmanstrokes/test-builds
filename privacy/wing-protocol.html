Privacy Policy: Wing Protocol

Developer: Diddlerrr
Contact: mscofield1588@gmail.com
App: Wing Protocol (Android package com.diddlerrr.wingprotocol) and its web versions (our website and game portals such as CrazyGames and Poki)
Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Wing Protocol ("the game", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. Wing Protocol is a mech survival shooter for players aged 13 and older.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • We use Sentry for crash reports and PostHog for anonymous play statistics. Neither is linked to your identity, and both are switched off if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • The Android app can show an optional rewarded video ad from Google AdMob when you choose to watch one for a Sponsor Crate. It never shows ads you did not ask for. On CrazyGames and Poki, the portal's own ad service can show an optional rewarded video when you choose to watch one, and Poki can also show an ad between runs.
  • There are no real-money purchases.
  • We do not sell personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your progress (unlocked mechs, gear, credits, keys, cores and shards, mission progress, best Tower floor, crate timers and settings such as sound and graphics quality) is saved in local storage on your device. We never receive it and cannot view or restore it. You can erase it by clearing the app's storage or your browser's site data, or by uninstalling the app. If Android backup is on, Android may include this data in your own Google account backup, which you and Google control.

2. Crash reports (Sentry)

If the game hits an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., US servers). A report contains the error message and stack trace, the game page address with any query string removed, your browser or WebView user-agent (browser, OS and device type), the game name, and up to 20 recent technical events leading up to the error. We configure Sentry not to send default personal data: we remove user and server fields, filter out anything you type, and replace any email address in the report with "[email]". Like any web request, your IP address reaches Sentry's servers when the report is delivered, but we have told Sentry not to attach personal data to reports. We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs. Learn more: https://sentry.io/privacy/

3. Anonymous play statistics (PostHog)

The game sends anonymous usage events to PostHog (PostHog, Inc., US servers): page views and page leaves, plus game events such as starting or finishing a run. Each event carries a random ID stored in your device's local storage, the game name, the page address with any query string removed, and basic browser and device details. We never identify you or create a profile, and we do not record your screen or sessions. We do not capture clicks or anything you type, and we do not record referrers. We ask PostHog not to store IP addresses. We use these statistics only to understand how the game is played and improve it. Learn more: https://posthog.com/privacy

Opting out: if your browser or device sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the game does not load Sentry or PostHog at all. Clearing the app's storage or your site data resets the random ID.

4. Ads

  • Android app (Google AdMob): the app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Ads are rewarded only: a video loads and plays only when you tap to watch one for a Sponsor Crate, and there are no banner, pop-up or between-level ads. To serve and measure these ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (such as device model, OS version and app version), and ad interaction data (such as whether an ad was shown, completed or tapped). Before any ad is requested, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland); you can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices in the Store. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings. Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • CrazyGames: when the game runs on CrazyGames, it loads the CrazyGames SDK. Ads are rewarded only: a video plays only when you tap to watch one for a reward. CrazyGames and its ad partners deliver these ads under the CrazyGames privacy policy.
  • Poki: when the game runs on Poki, it loads the Poki SDK. Poki can show a rewarded video when you choose to watch one, and an ad at a natural break before you start a new run, never during play. Poki delivers these ads under the Poki privacy policy.
  • Our own website: no ads.

We do not receive any personal information from these ad services.

5. Fonts loaded from Google

When online, the game downloads its typefaces (Rajdhani and IBM Plex Sans) from Google Fonts. This sends your IP address and basic browser information to Google so the fonts can be delivered. It includes no game data or identifiers from us. Google states that it does not use Google Fonts requests to build profiles or target ads. Offline, the game uses your device's built-in fonts. Learn more: https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy

6. In-app purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Crates are opened with in-game currency earned by playing, and the drop odds are shown on every crate. If purchases are ever added, payments will be processed by Google Play, we will never receive your payment details, and we will update this policy first.

7. Permissions

The Android app requests internet access, used for the fonts, crash reports, play statistics and ads described above, and the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), used only by Google AdMob for rewarded ads. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. The data described above is processed for us by Sentry, PostHog, Google Fonts and, in the Android app, Google AdMob, and on game portals by the portal operator. We share it with no one else, except where the law requires us to.

9. Data retention and security

Progress stays on your device until you delete it. Crash reports are kept in Sentry for up to 90 days. Play statistics are kept in PostHog for as long as our PostHog plan retains them, and we delete them when they are no longer needed. All of the game's network requests use HTTPS. We run no servers of our own for this game.

10. Children

The game is intended for players 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, email us and we will delete whatever we can identify.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Game data: clear the app's storage or your site data, or uninstall the app.
  • Ads (Android): ads only play when you tap to watch one. Use Ad privacy choices in the Store to change your consent, and your Android settings to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Statistics and crash reports: turn on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control to stop them.
  • Requests: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access or delete information about you. Because the data is anonymous we usually cannot link it to you, but you can email mscofield1588@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

12. Changes

We will update this policy whenever the game's data practices change. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
