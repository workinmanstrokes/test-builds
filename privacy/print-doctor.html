Privacy Policy – Print Doctor

Effective October 23, 2026 · Developer: DowntownDiddlerrr

Print Doctor is an offline 3D-print troubleshooting app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it on Android or on the web.

Information we collect

None. Print Doctor collects no data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics or crash reporting, no servers, and no third-party telemetry (including Firebase, Sentry or PostHog). We do not sell or share your information because we do not receive it.

Your print log and photos

If you keep a print log, your entries — including any photo you take or choose, plus the settings and notes you enter — are stored only on your device in IndexedDB. They are never transmitted. Uninstalling the app, clearing the app's storage, or clearing site data deletes this information.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo picker are used only when you choose to attach a photo to a log entry, through your device's standard file chooser. The app does not access your camera or photos in the background, and it does not add an Android CAMERA permission of its own.

Permissions

The Android app includes the INTERNET permission because that is the default in Capacitor, the web-view framework the app is built with. Print Doctor itself runs offline and does not use the network to send or receive your data. It does not request location, microphone, contacts, or general storage access.

Children

Print Doctor is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
