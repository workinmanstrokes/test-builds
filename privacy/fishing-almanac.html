Fishing Almanac Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Developer: Diddlerrr

Fishing Almanac is a fishing planner and catch log made by Diddlerrr. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it on Android or on the web. There are no ads, no accounts and no sale of data.

Stored only on your device

Your trips, catch log and trophy room, private catches and their GPS waypoints, saved private spots, gear list, saved waters, profile name and photo, bite-alert choices and cached map tiles are kept in the app's local storage on your device. We cannot see them. You can remove them by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, clearing site data in your browser, or uninstalling the app.

Public catches (optional leaderboard)

Catches are private by default. If you turn off Private for a catch and it is the biggest of its species, it is posted to a shared leaderboard that other users can see. That entry contains the species, weight, length, lure, depth, catch photo (if you added one), the name and profile photo you chose, the water name, the weather and moon phase at the time, and the date. Public entries never include your GPS waypoint. They are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). The app signs in to Firebase anonymously (a random ID, no email or account) so the leaderboard can be protected; this ID is not linked to your identity. To remove a public entry, email us.

Location

When you tap Near me, My position or Pin me here, the app asks for your device location. If precise location isn't available, it may estimate an approximate location from your IP address using ipapi.co. Your coordinates are used on your device and sent only to the public data services below to fetch information for that spot. We do not store or track your location on any server.

Third-party data services

To show reports, the app sends the place name or coordinates you're looking at (and, like any web request, your IP address) to:

  • Open-Meteo: weather and pressure
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim and Overpass): place search and nearby waters
  • U.S. Geological Survey Water Services: stream gauges
  • Esri ArcGIS: satellite imagery and Michigan DNR depth contours
  • ipapi.co: approximate location, only when device location isn't available
  • Google Firebase: the public leaderboard described above

These services handle requests under their own privacy policies. None of them receive your catch log, trips or private spots.

Permissions

The Android app requests internet access (to load maps, weather and gauges) and location (approximate and precise, only when you use a location feature). Adding a catch or profile photo hands off to the system camera or photo picker; the app itself does not request camera, microphone or contacts permission.

Children

Fishing Almanac is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has posted to the leaderboard, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
