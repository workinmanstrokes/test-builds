Privacy Policy – NHL94-Remake (working title)

Effective November 14, 2026 · Developer: Diddlerrr

NHL94-Remake (working title) is an offline hockey game by developer Diddlerrr. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The game collects no personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no servers. It shares nothing with third parties. We do not sell or share your information because we do not receive it.

Game progress

Game progress, if any, is stored only on your device. It is never transmitted. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage deletes this information.

Children

Nothing is collected from anyone, including children.
