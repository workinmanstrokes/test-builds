Diddlerrr · Soundboard

Privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Soundboard is a soundboard made by Diddlerrr. You tap a pad and a short sound plays. This page says exactly what the app does with information. It does not use ads, analytics, or an account.

What the app does

  • Plays a built-in pack of original synthesized sound effects. Those sounds are part of the app. They are not recordings of people.
  • Lets you record your own sound with the microphone and place it on a pad, if you choose to.
  • Lets you search the Internet Archive for short audio that has a clear free license (public domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA), preview it, and save a copy onto a pad.
  • Lets you rename, delete, or download a pad, and chain up to eight pads into one tap.
  • Plays sound through this device’s speaker.

Microphone

The microphone is used only after you open Record and tap Record. It is not used at launch, and it is not used for the built-in pads. The recording is kept in the app’s private storage on this device. Soundboard does not upload, share, or send recordings to Diddlerrr or to anyone else. If you deny microphone access, the app tells you and the rest of the board keeps working. You can record again later if you allow the microphone in system settings.

On Android the app declares internet access (sound search, and opening this page in the browser), the microphone, and the ordinary audio-settings permission the system WebView asks for at the same time as the microphone so a recording can start. The system asks you before the microphone is used. No other permissions are declared.

Internet and sound search

The built-in sounds play with no network. When you search, the words you type are sent over HTTPS to the Internet Archive at archive.org so it can return results. If you preview or save a result, the app downloads that audio file from archive.org. A saved file is then stored on this device. Diddlerrr does not receive your search words, the files, or a copy of what you play. The Internet Archive’s own policy applies to the request they receive.

Results without a public-domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA license are not shown and are not saved. When a clip is saved, the app keeps the license name and attribution with the pad.

The in-app Privacy link opens this page in the browser. That request is between your device and the site that hosts this page. Diddlerrr does not add tracking parameters to it.

On-device storage

Pad names, colours, chains, the volume setting, recordings, and audio you save are stored only on the device (local storage and IndexedDB in the app’s private data). They are not synced to a Diddlerrr server. There is no account.

Ads and analytics

Soundboard does not show ads. It does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting services. Diddlerrr does not collect usage statistics from the app.

What we do not collect

Diddlerrr does not collect your name, contacts, photos, location, or the contents of your recordings. The app does not ask you to sign in.

Deletion

Reset board, inside More, deletes the pads, recordings, saved clips, and chains stored by the app on this device and restores the original starter sounds. Uninstalling the app removes its private storage as well.

Children

Soundboard is not directed at children under 13, and Diddlerrr does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
