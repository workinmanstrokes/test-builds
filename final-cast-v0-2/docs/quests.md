# Final Cast — 12 quests (v0.2 rewrite)

Every quest unlocks something new — tackle, a lure, a water, or a mechanic. Any quest that was only "catch X more fish" with no new choice or reward was cut or rewritten. Difficulty: 1 = first hour, 5 = endgame.

| # | Objective | New unlock / mechanic | Water | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cast your line and catch your first fish (idle line does the work) | Idle line + first rod | Millpond | 1 |
| 2 | Land your first hand-fought fish: hookset inside the bite window | Hand-fishing loop + bite cue | Millpond | 1 |
| 3 | Try all three retrieve speeds (slow / medium / fast) and note which one bites | Lure retrieves at three speeds | Millpond | 2 |
| 4 | Fish at three worm depths and catch one fish from the deep | Worm depth (shallow / mid / deep) | Millpond | 2 |
| 5 | Catch a fish from each of the three Millpond spots | Fishing-spot choice | Millpond | 2 |
| 6 | Unlock and equip your first lure | Lure unlocks + tackle shop | Millpond | 2 |
| 7 | Upgrade your rod, then land a large fish | Rod upgrades | Cedar Creek | 3 |
| 8 | Fish Cedar Creek at dusk and meet the Silver King | Legend encounters + time of day | Cedar Creek | 3 |
| 9 | Unlock the Ember Lake water by completing the Cedar Creek set | New water: Ember Lake | Ember Lake | 4 |
| 10 | Catch the Emberfin at the Hot Spring Inlet at night | Legend tied to one spot + time | Ember Lake | 4 |
| 11 | Complete the collection: one of every common fish across all three waters | Collection log + completion rewards | All waters | 5 |
| 12 | Land the Old Mossback at the Sunken Log at dawn — the third legend | Third legend + endgame unlock | Millpond | 5 |

## Quests cut or rewritten

- Any v0.1 quest that read as "catch X more fish" with no new choice or reward was rewritten above (quests 3, 4, 5 each add a mechanic instead of a count) or cut. The v0.1 quest list is in the audit file — confirm each cut quest against it during the line-by-line audit.

## Endgame — after quest 12 and legend 3

- **Collection completion**: finish the collection log; each completed set pays a coin bonus and a title.
- **Harder legend variants**: each legend returns as a "golden" variant with a tighter bite/hookset window.
- **Daily / spot rotation**: one spot per day is "hot" (better odds, noted in the game); legend spots rotate weekly.
- If none of those is chosen, the game ends cleanly with a completion screen and a free-play mode.

## Monetization (explicit decision)

**Free, no ads.** Recorded in the game settings. If this ever changes, the decision is re-recorded here and in Settings — never silently.
