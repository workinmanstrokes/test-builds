Developer: Diddlerrr

App: Who's the Brainrot? (Android package com.diddlerrr.whosthebrainrot, and the web version of the same game)
Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the Who's the Brainrot? game ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. It is a pass-and-play party game for players aged 13 and older.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up, no analytics. We never ask for your email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • Player names you type, scores and settings stay on your device.
  • The Android app uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded video ads (watch an ad to unlock a character pack for 24 hours). Google collects some device information to show those ads, as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not run any servers for this game.

1. Information stored only on your device

The player names you enter, scores, the packs you have unlocked and when they expire, recently used characters (so they don't repeat too often) and settings (sound, vibration, clip mode, game options) are saved in the app's local storage on your device. We never receive this information and cannot see or restore it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. Please use first names or nicknames for players. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your own Google account backup, which is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch to unlock a character pack for 24 hours. There are no forced, pop-up or mid-game ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), your IP address (which may be used to estimate your approximate location), ad interaction data, and device, app and diagnostic information. Google uses this information to deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads, to personalise ads where permitted, and to prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, or under US state privacy laws), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Web and game-portal versions

When you play the web version on a game portal (such as Poki, CrazyGames or itch.io), the portal may show its own optional rewarded ads and handles data under its own privacy policy. Our game code itself does not send any data anywhere.

4. In-app purchase (if offered)

The app may offer an optional one-time purchase through Google Play that unlocks all packs. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment card or billing details. The app asks Google Play whether you own the purchase so it can unlock the packs.

5. Permissions

The app uses internet access (to load optional ads) and vibration for haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings. Google's ad SDK uses the Advertising ID permission. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share personal information for money. Information is collected by Google (AdMob) only to provide ads as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Data retention and security

On-device data stays until you delete it. AdMob data is retained by Google according to Google's policies and is encrypted in transit. We operate no servers and hold no copy of your data.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and information was collected, contact us and we will help have it deleted.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Ads and consent: Settings > Privacy options (where shown), or reset/delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Game data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Requests: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing of information about you. Email mscofield1588@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
