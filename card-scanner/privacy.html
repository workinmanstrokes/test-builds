Card Scanner Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026. Developer: Diddlerrr. Contact: mscofield1588@gmail.com.

Audience

Card Scanner is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

What we collect

We do not require an account and do not collect names, emails or location. Your collection, photos and grading results are stored only on your device.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to photograph cards you choose to scan. When you use Identify, the card photo is sent to our identification service (which uses an AI vision provider such as xAI) solely to recognize the card, and is not stored by us afterward.

Third-party services

To look up card details and prices the app sends card names/numbers (not personal data) to TCGdex, Scryfall, YGOPRODeck, and, when enabled, eBay and SportsCardsPro. These services have their own privacy policies.

Sharing and ads

We do not sell or share personal data. The app contains no ads or analytics.

Deleting data

Clear the app's data or uninstall it to delete everything stored on your device.

Changes

We will post updates on this page. Questions: mscofield1588@gmail.com.
