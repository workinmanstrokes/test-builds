    How this is measured

    Centering border widths are measured on 41 lines per side on the straightened card and shown as left/right and top/bottom ratios.

    Print lines are straight, faint lines that run across the card, including through the border.

    Scratches are thin, bright streaks that catch the light. Busy holo patterns are checked less strictly.

    Edges and corners are checked for whitening, chipping and rounding along the actual cut edge.

    Not the right card?

    Prices come from TCGdex (TCGplayer, Cardmarket) and Scryfall, shown with the date each source reports. Recent sales are completed auctions. “Listed now” shows asking prices of active eBay listings, which are not sales. Card Pre­Grade isn't affiliated with any of these services.

    About Card PreGrade

    Card PreGrade estimates a trading card's condition from photos: centering, corners, edges and surface flaws (print lines, scratches, whitening). It runs entirely on your phone.

    It gives an estimate, not an official grade, and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any grading company.

    Privacy

    • Card photos are analyzed on your device and are never uploaded.
    • To identify and price a card, only the text read from it (name, number, set) is sent to free card databases (TCGdex, Scryfall, Wikipedia) and, for recent sales, to our sales lookup.
    • Recent scans (a small thumbnail and scores) are stored only on this phone. You can clear them below.
    • Anonymous crash reports and usage counts help fix bugs. They contain no photos and no personal info.
